# How to Use the AI Assistant

The AI Assistant is the first thing you see when you sign in to TurboDocx. You type what you need in plain words, and it does the work for you: it finds documents, answers questions about them, drafts documents from your templates, and gets signature requests ready.

You stay in control. **Nothing is sent and nothing is changed until you approve it.**

## What You'll Learn

- 💬 **Ask a question** and read the answer
- 🧠 **See how the assistant worked** by opening its activity rows
- 📎 **Attach a Word or PDF file** to your message
- 📄 **Turn a document into a template**, and update it later
- ✅ **Approve or decline** anything the assistant wants to send
- 🗂️ **Manage your conversations**: start a new one, rename it, or delete it
- ✏️ **Stay within the message length limit**

:::note
The AI Assistant is in beta. It can make mistakes, so check its work before you rely on it.
:::

## Step 1: Open the assistant

The assistant lives on your home page. You land there every time you sign in.

To get back to it from anywhere else in TurboDocx:

1. Look at the narrow sidebar on the far left of the screen.
2. Click the **house** icon.

![The left sidebar with the house Home icon highlighted, and the assistant home page open](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/open_home.png)

:::tip
The two small buttons at the very top of the sidebar switch what the sidebar shows. **Browse** (the grid icon) shows the pages of the app, such as Home, Dashboard and Templates. **Chat** (the speech bubbles icon) shows your conversations. If you don't see the house icon, click **Browse** first.

![The Browse and Chat switch at the top of the sidebar highlighted](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/browse_chat_switch.png)
:::

## Step 2: Ask a question

1. Click the large text box in the middle of the page.
2. Type your question in your own words. For example: *Which of my documents are still waiting on a signature?*

![The assistant text box highlighted on the home page](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/composer.png)

3. Click the round **arrow** button in the bottom right corner of the box, or press **Enter**.

![The text box with a question typed in and the send arrow button highlighted](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/send_question.png)

The assistant answers below your question.

:::tip
Not sure what to ask? Click one of the suggestions under the text box, such as **What's waiting on a signature?** or **What needs my attention?**
:::

## Step 3: See how the assistant worked (optional)

Above each answer you will see a few short grey rows. They tell you what the assistant did to get there:

- A row with a **check mark**, such as *Found your signature requests*, is a step it took in your account.
- A row that says **Thought for** a few seconds is where it worked out its answer.

These rows are folded shut so they stay out of your way. To see what is inside one, click it.

![An answer with the Thought for 14s row highlighted above it](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/answer_thinking_row.png)

The row opens and shows the assistant's notes. Click the same row again to fold it back up.

![The Thought for row opened, showing the assistant's notes above the answer (blurred in this picture)](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/thinking_open.png)

:::note
You never need to open these rows. They are there for when you want to check how the assistant reached an answer.
:::

## Step 4: Attach a Word or PDF file

You can give the assistant a document to read, for example a contract you want to reuse or a copy a client sent back.

1. Click the **paperclip** button in the bottom left corner of the text box.

![The text box with the paperclip Attach a document button highlighted](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/attach_button.png)

2. Pick the file on your computer. You can also drag the file from your computer and drop it onto the text box.
3. The file appears inside the text box. Type what you want done with it, then send your message.

![A Word file attached in the text box, with a request typed underneath](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/attached_file.png)

To remove a file before you send, click the small **✕** on the file.

:::note
- You can attach **Word (.docx)** and **PDF** files.
- Each file can be up to **25 MB**.
- You can attach up to **3 files** to one message.
:::

:::tip
If the file is a copy of one of your documents that someone sent back with changes, the assistant opens it for review. See [How to Review a Returned Copy](./TurboDocx-Templating/How-to-Review-a-Returned-Copy.md#review-from-the-ai-assistant).
:::

## Step 5: Turn a document into a template

This is one of the most useful things the assistant can do. Attach a finished document, such as a signed agreement, and ask it to make a reusable template.

1. Attach the document (see Step 4).
2. Type *Turn this agreement into a reusable template.* and send it.

The assistant finds the details that change from one document to the next, such as the client name, the dates and the fee, and turns each one into a field. A **Template preview** opens on the right side of the screen.

![The Template preview panel listing each field, with what it was and what it becomes](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/template_preview.png)

In the preview, each row shows the original text on the left (**was "Harbor Peak Analytics LLC"**) and the field it becomes on the right (`{client_name}`). Under **Worth a look**, the assistant points out anything you should double check. Click **Show original** at the top to compare with the file you uploaded.

3. If something is wrong, type what to change in the text box, for example *Rename the monthly_fee field to fee*. The assistant makes a new preview.
4. When the preview looks right, click **Save template** on the card in the conversation. You can also click **Save as template** at the bottom of the preview.

![The Save this as a template card with the Save template button highlighted](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/save_template_card.png)

Nothing is saved until you click. When it is saved, the assistant tells you how many fields the template has. Click **Open** to see the template, or **Start a document** to make your first document from it straight away.

![The Saved card showing the new template, with an Open button and a Start a document button](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/template_saved.png)

### Changing a template you already saved

If you ask for a change after saving, you don't get a second copy of the template. The card says **Update the saved template?** and the button says **Update template**. Clicking it saves your changes as a new version of the same template.

![The Update the saved template card with the Update template button highlighted](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/update_template_card.png)

## Step 6: Approve or decline what the assistant wants to send

The assistant never sends an email, a signature request or a quote on its own, and it never voids a document on its own. When you ask it to do something like that, it gets everything ready and then shows you an **approval card**.

1. Read the card. It shows exactly what will happen, the document, and who it goes to.
2. Click **Send now** to go ahead, or **Don't send** to stop.

![An approval card asking Send this for signature, with the Send now and Don't send buttons highlighted. Email addresses are blurred.](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/approval_card.png)

The card then shrinks to a single line that records your decision, such as **Sent** or **Not sent**.

![The approval card after declining, now a single Not sent line](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/approval_not_sent.png)

:::tip
Declining does not delete anything. In the example above, the signature request stays prepared and waiting, so you can change it or send it later.
:::

## Step 7: Manage your conversations

Every conversation is saved, so you can come back to it later.

### Start a new conversation

Click the **pencil and paper** icon near the top of the sidebar. It starts a fresh, empty conversation. You can also click **New conversation** when the sidebar is open.

![The New conversation button highlighted in the left sidebar](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/new_conversation_button.png)

### Find an earlier conversation

1. Make sure the sidebar is set to **Chat** (see the tip in Step 1).
2. Move your mouse over the sidebar. It opens and shows your **Recent conversations**, newest first.
3. Click a conversation to open it again.

![The open sidebar with the Recent conversations list highlighted](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/rail_open.png)

### Rename or delete a conversation

1. Move your mouse over the conversation in the list. Two small buttons appear on the right: a **pencil** (rename) and a **bin** (delete).

![A conversation in the list with the pencil rename button and the bin delete button highlighted](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/rail_rename_delete.png)

2. To rename it, click the **pencil**, type the new name and press **Enter**. Press **Escape** to cancel.

![The conversation name being edited in the sidebar](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/rail_rename_typing.png)

3. To delete it, click the **bin**, then click **Delete** to confirm.

![The Delete this conversation dialog with the Delete button highlighted](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/delete_confirm.png)

Deleting a conversation removes it from your list. It does not delete any document, template or signature request the assistant made for you.

## Message length limit

A single message can be up to **8,000 characters** long. That is roughly three pages of text.

When you get close to the limit, a counter appears next to the send button. If you go over, the counter turns red and the send button stops working. Point at the send button to see how much to cut.

![A long message with the red 8,150 / 8,000 counter highlighted, and the Too long: shorten it to 8,000 characters tip](/img/how_to_use_the_ai_assistant/too_long.png)

:::tip
Have a long document? Attach it as a file instead of pasting it in. Attached files don't count toward the 8,000 characters.
:::

## Troubleshooting

**The assistant is not on my home page.** AI features are switched off for your organization, or your plan does not include them. Your home page then shows a greyed-out preview of the assistant with a short message. If you are an administrator, click **Turn on AI in settings** on that message. Otherwise, ask your administrator.

**I don't see the house icon in the sidebar.** The sidebar is showing your conversations. Click **Browse** (the grid icon) at the top of the sidebar.

**The send button is greyed out.** Either the text box is empty, a file is still uploading, or the message is over 8,000 characters. Point at the button to see which.

**The assistant said it would send something, but nothing was sent.** That is expected. Look for the approval card in the conversation and click **Send now**.
