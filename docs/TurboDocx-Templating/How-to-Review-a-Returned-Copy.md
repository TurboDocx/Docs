# How to Review a Returned Copy

You sent a document to a client. They sent a Word copy back with their edits and comments. This guide shows you how to go through every change in TurboDocx, decide what to keep, and save the result.

TurboDocx finds **every** change, including edits the client made without turning on Track Changes in Word.

## What You'll Learn

- 📤 **Upload the copy** you got back and compare it with your document
- 🔍 **Read the review page**: the changes, the comments and the filter
- ✅ **Accept or reject each change**, and undo a decision
- 💬 **Resolve or remove each comment**
- ⌨️ **Use keyboard keys** to move faster
- 💾 **Save** as a new version of your document or as a new document
- 🤖 **Start the same review from the AI Assistant**

## Before you start

- You need the returned copy as a **Word (.docx)** file.
- The original document must be one you made in TurboDocx (a deliverable).

## Step 1: Open the document the client edited

1. Open **Deliverables** from the left sidebar.
2. Click the document you sent to the client. Its preview page opens.
3. Click **Manage** on the right, above the preview.

![The deliverable preview page with the Manage button highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/manage_button.png)

4. Click **Review a returned copy**.

![The Manage menu open, with Review a returned copy highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/review_returned_copy_item.png)

## Step 2: Upload the copy you got back

1. In the **Review a returned copy** window, click **Upload a file**.
2. Pick the Word file the client sent you.

![The Review a returned copy window with the Upload a file button highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/upload_a_file.png)

:::tip
If the file is in Google Drive or SharePoint, use those buttons instead of **Upload a file**. They only appear if your organization uses them.
:::

TurboDocx checks which document and which version the copy came from, and how many tracked changes and comments it has.

3. Check the sentence it shows. For example: *This is a copy of Contoso Ltd SOW, made from version 2.* If you picked the wrong file, click **Change**.
4. Click **Compare**.

![The window confirming which document the copy came from, with the Compare button highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/compare.png)

:::note
If TurboDocx can't tell which document the copy belongs to, it asks you to choose one. If the file does not look like the document you started from, it warns you before comparing.

If nothing changed, it tells you the copy matches your document, and there is nothing to review.
:::

## Step 3: Read the review page

The review page opens. Your document is on the left, drawn as a page. Every change and every comment has a **card** on the right, next to the part of the page it belongs to.

![The review page with the document on the left and the change and comment cards on the right](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/review_page.png)

The line at the top tells you what was compared and how much changed. For example: *Compared with version 2 · 2 changes and 2 comments: 1 tracked by Client Counsel, 1 edited without tracking.*

![The summary line at the top of the review page highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/review_header.png)

On the page:

- Added text is **underlined** and removed text is ~~struck through~~.
- A **solid** bar in the left margin marks a change the client tracked in Word.
- A **dashed** bar marks a change they made **without tracking**. TurboDocx found it by comparing the two files.

Each change card tells you:

- What kind of change it is, for example **Field: Total fee** or **Edited without tracking**.
- Who made it and when, if Word recorded that.
- A short summary in plain words, marked with the TurboDocx logo.
- The exact change, for example *Replace "$35,000.00" with "$42,000.00"*.
- What happens when you save, for example *Total fee becomes $42,000.00 when you save.*

![A change card for the Total fee field, showing the author, the summary, the exact change and what happens on save](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/card_fee.png)

:::caution
The short summaries are written by AI and can be wrong. The marked changes on the page and the *Replace ... with ...* line are always exact. When in doubt, trust those.
:::

## Step 4: Accept or reject each change

1. On a change card, click **Accept** to keep the client's change, or **Reject** to keep your original wording.

![A change card with the Accept button highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/accept.png)

2. The card shrinks to a single line that says what you decided, for example **Accepted Total fee**.

![The decided card now showing Accepted Total fee on one line](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/accepted_line.png)

Changed your mind? Click **Undo** on that line, or **Undo** at the bottom of the page to undo your last decision.

![A decided line with its Undo button highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/undo.png)

:::tip
To decide many changes at once, use the **Accept all** button at the top of the page. Click the small arrow next to it to reject them all instead. It only acts on the changes that match the filter you picked (see Step 6).
:::

:::tip
You can also **right-click** a marked change on the page to accept or reject it from a menu.
:::

## Step 5: Resolve or remove each comment

Comment cards have two buttons. They do very different things:

| Button | What it does to the saved file |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| **Resolve** | Keeps the comment in the file and marks it as resolved, the same as **Resolve** in Word. It changes no text. |
| **Remove comment** | Deletes the comment from the file. It changes no text either. |

1. Click **Resolve** when you have dealt with the comment and want to keep a record of it.

![A comment card asking for fifteen business days, with the Resolve button highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/resolve.png)

2. Click **Remove comment** when the comment is not worth keeping.

![A comment card with the Remove comment button highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/remove_comment.png)

:::note
A comment often asks for a change, such as *Can we make this fifteen business days?* Resolving it does **not** make that change for you. If you agree, make the edit in your document afterwards.
:::

## Step 6: Show only some changes (optional)

On a long document, use the **filter** at the top left to see one kind of change at a time.

![The filter dropdown showing All changes highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/filter.png)

Click it and pick what to show. Each option shows how many changes it has. For example:

- **Not decided yet**: what is left to do.
- **Edited without tracking**: changes the client did not mark in Word. Worth a careful look.
- **Fields**: changes to values such as a fee, a date or a client name.
- **Comments**: only the comments.

![The filter open, listing All changes, Not decided yet, Edited without tracking, Fields, Template wording and Comments with their counts](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/filter_open.png)

Use the **up** and **down** arrows next to the filter to move from one change to the next.

## Step 7: Move faster with the keyboard (optional)

Click anywhere on the document page first. Then you can use these keys:

| Key | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| **J** | Go to the next change you haven't decided |
| **K** | Go to the previous change you haven't decided |
| **A** | Accept the current change, or resolve the current comment |
| **R** | Reject the current change, or remove the current comment |
| **U** | Undo your last decision |

The current card has a coloured bar down its left side.

![The current card highlighted after pressing J](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/keyboard_current.png)

Press **R** and that change is rejected. Its card shrinks to a **Rejected** line, just as if you had clicked the button.

![The card after pressing R, now a single Rejected Template wording line](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/rejected_line.png)

## Step 8: Save your review

Once every change and comment is decided, the bottom right of the page changes from *Decide N more to save* to a **Review and save** button.

1. Click **Review and save**.

![The bottom of the review page with the Review and save button highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/review_and_save.png)

2. A summary opens. It shows how many changes you accepted and rejected, and which fields will get a new value.

![The Save your review window showing 1 accepted, 1 rejected and the Total fee field that updates](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/save_recap.png)

3. Under **Save as**, pick one:

   - **New version of** your document. This is the usual choice. Your document keeps its name and history, and the reviewed copy becomes its newest version.

   ![The New version option and the Save new version button highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/save_as_version.png)

   - **New document**. Saves the reviewed copy as a separate document, with a name you choose. It won't be linked to the original.

   ![The New document option selected, with a name field below it](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/save_as_new_document.png)

4. Click **Save new version** (or **Save as new document**).

Nothing is saved until you click this button. If you want to look again first, click **Back to review**.

TurboDocx opens the saved document and confirms it, for example *Saved as a new version of Contoso Ltd SOW*.

![The deliverable page after saving, with the Saved as a new version message](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/saved_version.png)

:::note
If someone saved a newer version of the document after the client's copy was made, **New version** is not available. TurboDocx tells you, and offers a **Compare with your latest version** link so you can review the copy against the newest version instead.
:::

## Review from the AI Assistant

You can start the same review from the [AI Assistant](../How-to-Use-the-AI-Assistant.md) on your home page, without finding the document first.

1. On your home page, click the **paperclip** button and attach the copy you got back.
2. Type something like *The client sent this back. What did they change?* and send it.

![The assistant text box with the returned copy attached and the send button highlighted](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/chat_attach.png)

The assistant finds the document it came from, compares the two, and sums up what changed. The same review opens on the right side of the screen, with the same cards, buttons and keys as above.

![The review open beside the conversation, with the Review view selected](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/chat_review_pane.png)

To review on a full page instead, click the **Open full page** button (two arrows) at the top of the panel.

![The Open full page button highlighted at the top of the review panel](/img/how_to_review_a_returned_copy/chat_open_full_page.png)

:::tip
Click **Print** at the top of the panel to see the copy as a printable page, with the decisions you have made so far.
:::

## Troubleshooting

**"Review a returned copy" is greyed out in the Manage menu.** You don't have permission to edit that document. Ask an administrator.

**The upload is refused.** Only Word (.docx) files can be reviewed. If you have a PDF or a Google Doc, save it as a Word file first. Very large files, and files with macros, are also refused.

**TurboDocx says it does not look like the document.** You may have picked the wrong document or the wrong file. Click **Choose another document**, or **Compare anyway** if you are sure.

**I can't click Review and save.** Some changes or comments are still undecided. Pick **Not decided yet** in the filter to find them.
