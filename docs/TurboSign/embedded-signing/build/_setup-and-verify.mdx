An admin must have completed [Set up your organization](../set-up-your-organization.md): **Enable identity verification** is on (it is the master switch for embedded signing), and your app's origin is under **Allowed embedding domains**.

This guide verifies each signer with an **email passcode**. Every embedded signer needs a verification method unless you use the sender override while testing. To use SMS, your own identity provider, or the override, see [Identity verification](../../identity-verification/index.md).
