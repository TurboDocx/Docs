# Managing Categories

Categories keep your catalog organised. Products, bundles and price books each have their own list of categories, and companies are classified by **industry**. You manage all four from one page.

## What You'll Accomplish

By the end of this guide, you will know how to:

- 🗂️ **Switch between the four category lists**
- ➕ **Add a category**
- ✏️ **Rename or delete** a category, and why some can't be deleted

<br/>

## The Categories Page

In the sidebar, open **TurboQuote** and click **Manage Categories**. The tabs switch between the four lists, each with a count:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Products** | Groups products in the catalog and on quotes. Quotes list items under these headings. |
| **Bundles** | Groups bundles in the catalog and in the **Add bundles** window. |
| **Price books** | Labels price books, like Standard, Partner or Enterprise. |
| **Industries** | Classifies the companies you sell to, like Healthcare or Finance. |

The **Used by** column shows how many products, bundles, price books or companies use each category. Categories that come with TurboQuote are tagged **Default**.

## Add a Category

**Instruction:**

1. Open the tab for the kind of category you want.
2. Click **Add product category** (or **Add bundle category**, **Add price book category**, **Add industry**).
3. Enter the **Name** and click **Add category**.

The category is available straight away. You can also create a category by typing a new name in the **Category** field of a product, bundle or price book. To add many at once, click **Import**; see [Bulk Importing from a Spreadsheet](./Bulk-Importing-from-a-Spreadsheet.md).

![The Add product category dialog](/img/turboquote/categories/03-add-category.png)

<br/>

## Rename or Delete a Category

Click the **⋮** button on a category and choose **Rename** or **Delete**. Renaming updates everything that uses the category.

A category can't be deleted when:

- it is a **Default** category, or
- something still uses it. Move those products, bundles, price books or companies to another category first.

In both cases **Delete** is greyed out; hover the **ⓘ** icon to see why.

![The category menu with Delete greyed out: "Default categories can't be deleted."](/img/turboquote/categories/02-category-actions.png)

<br/>

## Summary

1. One page holds the **product**, **bundle**, **price book** and **industry** categories
2. **Add** a category from its tab, or by typing a new name on a product, bundle or price book
3. **Default** categories and categories **in use** can't be deleted
