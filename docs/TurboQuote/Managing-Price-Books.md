# Managing Price Books

A price book is a discount tier, such as partner or nonprofit pricing. When a rep picks a price book on a quote, its products are repriced. Every new quote starts with your **default** price book.

## What You'll Accomplish

By the end of this guide, you will know how to:

- 📚 **Read the Price books page**
- 💲 **Set a discount** for every product in a book, with validity dates
- 🏷️ **Give single products their own price**, and reset them
- ⭐ **Choose the default** price book

<br/>

## The Price Books Page

In the sidebar, open **TurboQuote** and click **Price Books**. Each book shows its category, its discount, the dates it is valid, and whether it is offered on quotes. The default book is tagged **Default**. A book whose start date is in the future shows when it starts.

Click **New price book** to create one, or click a book's name to edit it.

![The Price books page with the New price book button](/img/turboquote/price-books/01-price-books.png)

<br/>

## Step 1: Fill In the General Tab

**Instruction:**

1. Enter the **Name**, a **Category** (type a name to create a new one) and an optional **Description**.
2. Enter the **Discount on every product**, as a percentage off the list price.
3. Set **Valid from**, and optionally **Valid to**. Leave **Valid to** empty to keep the book running.
4. Turn on **Default price book** to make new quotes start with this book. Only one book can be the default.
5. Leave **Offer it on quotes** on so reps can pick the book. Hidden books stay on the page, but reps can't pick them.

![The General tab with the book-wide discount and the validity dates](/img/turboquote/price-books/03-price-book-general.png)

<br/>

## Step 2: Give Products Their Own Price (Optional)

**Instruction:**

1. Open the **Product prices** tab. The number on the tab counts the products with their own price.
2. Find a product and change its **Discount**, as a percentage or an amount. The **Final price** updates as you type.
3. Click **Save changes**.

Every product follows the book's discount unless you give it its own here. A product with its own discount is tagged **Own price**; click the reset button at the end of its row to make it follow the book again.

![The Product prices tab with one product on its own price](/img/turboquote/price-books/04-product-prices.png)

To put every product back on the book's discount at once, click **Reset all** at the bottom of the list. It covers every product in the book, including any your search or category filter hides, so it clears the search and filter and says how many products it resets (for example, **Reset all 14 products to 10%**).

![1 product has its own price, and the Reset all button](/img/turboquote/price-books/05-reset-all.png)

<br/>

## Duplicate or Delete a Price Book

Click the **⋮** button on a book to **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Make default** (or **Remove as default**) or **Delete** it. **Duplicate** asks for the name of the copy.

The **default** book can't be deleted. Remove it as the default first, or make another book the default.

![The menu of the default price book. Delete is greyed out with the reason "It's the default. Remove it as the default first."](/img/turboquote/price-books/02-default-book.png)

<br/>

## Summary

1. A price book applies **one discount to every product**, between its valid dates
2. Give single products **their own price** on the **Product prices** tab, and **Reset all** to undo them
3. New quotes start with the **default** book, which can't be deleted
4. Turn off **Offer it on quotes** to hide a book from reps
